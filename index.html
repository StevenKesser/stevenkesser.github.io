About

I am an undergraduate student at the University of Georgia studying mathematics and statistics, with an emphasis in applied mathematics and a certificate in applied data science. My interests include statistical modeling, optimization, numerical analysis, machine learning, and applied research in health, neuroscience, and environmental data.

I am especially interested in using mathematical and statistical tools to understand complex real-world systems, including brain development, decision-making, and wildfire risk.
